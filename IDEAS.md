# Ideas for later updates

Nothing here is promised or scheduled. When you mention a new idea, I'll ask if it should go on this list. Each idea also gets a place in `PLAN.md` once you decide to build it.

1. **Album page view.** Keep the current album page as the default. Add a "View album" button that opens a virtual album where you turn pages, like a real Whitman or Dansco album.
2. **Report a problem button.** A small button in Options that opens a pre-filled note on your GitHub page, so problems reach me with the app version and what you were doing. Cheap to build; do it after the main features.
3. **First-time walkthrough.** A skippable tour for new users: how fast it is to add a coin step by step, how to view a coin, and cool features like adding an album and putting a coin in it.
4. **Shelf look on every page.** Make the other pages match the Home shelf, so it feels like real life. Keep a toggle between that and the current basic view (the basic view stays useful for long lists). **Includes the top-right menu:** style it like the cabinet too (wood or safe, following the style you pick), decided together with the other pages. **Your vision (2026-11-03):** the shelf was the whole idea. The shelf sits on top of a **cabinet**, and everything below it is the cabinet: Cost and Gain become **two side-by-side drawers**, Value over time becomes **one long drawer with two handles**, and so on down the page. Pages and menu names may need renaming back to cabinet words when this is built, so decide the names then. **Slabs vs raw (your note 2026-11-05):** draw slabbed coins differently from raw coins on the shelf so they stand out. Don't pad the shelf to look full.
5. **Different flip sizes.** When adding a coin, "In a flip" defaults to 2x2, with other flip sizes as options.
6. **Inventory printout.** A printable copy of the whole collection, like the insurance report but made as a backup you can keep on paper. Decide later whether it sits with Import/Export and Backup in Options.
7. **Reorganize the main menu.** Rearrange the top-right dropdown so related pages sit together. Your rough layout to start from (wording must make sense to a non-technical person *and* a collector or dealer):
    - **My Cabinet** (today's Home shelf)
    - **My Coins**, with **My Albums** and **Storage** tucked under it
    - **Wants List**
    - **Coin Value**: the value dropdowns live here; the Insurance Report may move here
    - **My Stacks**
    - **PCGS Photograde**
    - Options stays last. **Planned in PLAN.md Step 9.0.**
8. **Demo mode.** A built-in sample collection (lots of coins, rolls, 1 oz and 10 oz bars, a black box of wheat cents in 2x2 flips, partly filled albums) so a friend can try the app without seeing your real coins. Show off **photo fill** here (e.g. a ready-made example of a coin filled in from its photos), even while it's locked in the real app. Built from your backup plus made-up fill-in, kept separate from your real data. Best done once the features exist. Separately, a one-page **infographic** of the app's best features for you to show people (not in the app); **planned in PLAN.md session 25**, after the reviews and audit. Demo **planned in PLAN.md Step 9.1**.
9. **Tablet layout.** Make the pages fit a tablet screen well.
10. **Coin ID numbers.** An easy numbering/lettering system giving every coin a unique ID you can write on its flip, then search the ID to see where it is. **Planned in PLAN.md Step 7.0** (type code + number, e.g. MD-012).
11. **Cabinet drawers on Home.** The sections below the shelf (melt by metal, recently added, recently sold) drawn as cabinet drawers you pull open. Builds on the collapsible sections from the Fix list. Goes with the shelf look (idea 4). **Drawer layout:** Cost and Gain as two small drawers side by side; Value over time as one long drawer with two handles; Business stock, Sold results and the others as their own drawers.
12. **Add many identical coins to a container at once.** Add a "How many?" count, pick the container once, then tap each coin's real spot on the container's grid (coins sit in a certain order in real life). Each saves as its own coin. **Planned in PLAN.md Step 4.6.**
13. **AI test-user review.** Once the menu rework and demo mode exist, run one pretend first-time collector through the app (adding about 10 coins, trying each page). It writes an honest app-store-style review plus a note to you with: what was useful, what was confusing, what it wished for, how many taps tasks took, confusing words, anything that broke (with steps), what it thought of **photo fill** (or the locked "Coming soon" button), coins/albums it couldn't find (check against the Red Book, never guess), and a ranked wish list. Optionally a second run as a coin dealer to inform selling (Phase 7) and Seller Mode (Phase 8). Compare it with watching your friend's first 10 minutes. Keep it to one or two runs to save tokens. **Four test users, one run each:** (a) a first-time collector; (b) someone who only tracks a collection and never sells; (c) an amateur coin-show seller who prepares for a show with the app, then uses it to sell all weekend, then rates the experience; (d) a Whatnot live-stream collector/seller. (c) and (d) review the seller side, (a) and (b) overall usefulness. Each run is costly, so one chat each. **Two more users:** (e) an album filler who mostly fills Whitman or Dansco albums; (f) an heir who just inherited a box of coins and needs to inventory it and print the insurance report (watch for confusing words). Fold these scenarios into the runs: restoring a backup on a new phone, offline use at a coin show, importing a CSV, fixing a mistake (Undo, deleted coin, wrong grade), sharing with your partner. **Start with three runs:** (a) the first-time collector, (f) the heir, and (c) the coin-show seller. Run more only if new problems keep turning up; never more than about six. Maybe also a bars-and-rounds-only user (idea 25) and a low-vision/older user (text and button size) if budget allows.
14. **Free photo fill.** Use a free Google Gemini key in the photo-fill spot instead of a paid one (daily limits; check Google's terms on photo use first). Maybe also a free reader for slab labels.
15. **Business stock history.** Save one Business stock point per day (cost and value), like the personal collection's value history, with a small chart. Sales Reports then show the exact stock at the end of any period from the day it's built (earlier periods stay an estimate). Adds new saved data, so plan it on Opus and build on Sonnet after the Phase 8 review.
