# R3 Seller: report (app v0.77.2, Tue 2026-11-17)

Report only. No app code was changed. Three Opus sim agents ran at phone size on copies of the app (R3a coin-show seller, R3b Whatnot/eBay seller, R3c bookkeeper at tax time). Each was given the "already done / rejected" lists, IDEAS 99 to 116, R1 F1 to F26 and R2 N/P findings as known, plus the old seller reviews 03, 05, 06. Findings below are new, merged and de-duplicated (agent tags in brackets: S = R3a, W = R3b, T = R3c). Size guess: S small, M a feature, L changes saved data. Sev: A wrong data goes in, B lost, hidden or disagreeing numbers, C words or taps.

## Verdict
**The money math is solid.** All three agents checked by hand and got the same cent on Home, show page, Sales Reports, printable page, Sales CSV and coins CSV (fees, shipping, one 30 cent per order, holding period, month/quarter/year edges, restore of a mid-year backup). **The weak spots are the seams:** (1) the running show and its "Where" get filled in on sales they don't belong to (R3-1); (2) Personal, Business and Inherited coins mix in cases, reports and CSVs (R3-2, R3-3, R3-9, R3-10, R3-11); (3) Refund, Move back and Cancel put a coin back in the wrong state and have no partial refund or date (R3-4 to R3-6, R3-13). Nothing crashed, but see "Unverified" below.

## Unverified (worth one look in planning)
- **"Create report" button crashed the test browser twice** (R3c). Not reproduced by hand or traced; R3c built the report through the app's own function instead. It may be a test-browser problem, but nobody has proved that.

## Tap counts
- **Add a Business coin:** 12 to 14 (R3a), 14 to 15 with date, paid, source (R3c), 17 listed, slab 19 (R3b). Business is 2 to 3 folds deep.
- **Price one coin:** 1 (suggestion button) or 5 by typing; bulk 5.
- **Sell:** at asking 4; haggled 5; card with fee 6 to 7; one in a Whatnot stream 8; two as one order 12; a 4-coin deal 14; eBay with shipping and buyer 9 to 11.
- **Refund 3** (no question). **Return unsold 4** (no confirm, known F4). **Report:** this year 3, last year 4. **Tax CSV** 4 (5 last year). **Print** 6. **Seller Mode on:** 4 plus a scroll.
- **Too easy to hit:** Take off For sale, Move back to Personal, Refund (all act at once, about 8 px from Mark as sold); the round "+" floats over the right end of "Set asking price for all"; "Take out" sits right beside each case coin's name; "Return unsold coins" next to "Set asking price for all" (known).

## Old seller problems now fixed
Bulk asking price now follows "never below cost + 10%" (05 #1); paid-with carry-over (05 #4); one 30 cent per order; refunds keep the record; Supplies/Equipment/Subscriptions cost lines; whole-lot price; comma or "$" in the price box; Coin Value keeps Business apart (05 #7); Sales CSV has buyer, order, tracking and Type; Show and Business in the coins CSV.
