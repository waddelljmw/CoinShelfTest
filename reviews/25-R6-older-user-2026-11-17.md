# R6 Older / low-vision user: text size and taps (app v0.77.2, Tue 2026-11-17)

Report only. No app code, saved data, PLAN.md or IDEAS.md was changed (PLAN.md only gets its "done" tick in the docs PR). Run lean on purpose (session meter was at 77): no sub-agents. I measured the app myself with a phone-size browser (390x844 touch, plus 320x568 and a 200% zoom view) on `?demo=full`, on 8 pages and 6 pop-ups, and simulated bigger text (every font x1.3 and x1.5). Known lists given as "already known": R4's 61 ranked fixes (`reviews/23`), R5's 21 (`reviews/24`), IDEAS 99 to 116, and the done / rejected lists in PLAN.md. Only new findings are listed.

**Tags.** Sev: **A** wrong data goes in · **B** data hidden or lost · **C** hard to read, hard to tap, extra work. Size S/M/L as in R4. **Evidence:** VERIFIED-run (measured in the browser) · VERIFIED-code (file:line) · GUESS. The persona is a collector in their late 60s or 70s, reading glasses, bigger fingers, slower reader (like a family member set up in Simple mode).

**Yardsticks used:** text under 14 px is hard for older eyes; a tap target should be at least 44x44 px (Apple's and WCAG's comfortable size), and 24 px is the bare minimum (WCAG AA).

## Verdict
- **The app is readable for him, not for an older user.** On every page **about half the text is under 14 px** (Home 57%, My Coins 60%, album page 76%, Options 48%). The smallest text sits exactly where people read: the Home value labels (12 px), every help line (12 px), the album spot grade (11 px) and spot number (10 px).
- **There is no way to make it bigger.** Known as P5 (R4 rank 57). New: all sizes are fixed pixels, so on an iPhone the phone's own "Larger Text" setting does nothing here (GUESS for his phone model; VERIFIED-code that no size follows the phone). Pinch-zoom still works.
- **If text does get bigger, some screens break:** at 130% the Home page scrolls sideways and coin names are cut off; at 150% album value boxes cut "$1,089.0" and Options chips run off the screen. The coin page and the album grid hold up.
- **The help is the hardest thing to tap.** Each ⓘ is 23x15 px; the Add coin section headers are 22 px tall; back links are 22 px tall.
- **Two slips leave or change things without meaning to:** the eBay link touching each coin row on Coin Value, and the favourite star inside each coin row.
- **Already good (checked):** pinch-zoom allowed; typing boxes are 16 px (no jump-zoom on iPhone); the contrast test passes in light and dark; on/off switches are tappable across the whole row; main buttons are ~42 to 44 px; no swipe or long-press is ever needed; nothing scrolls sideways at a small 320 px phone width.

## How I used it that we didn't plan
1. **Pinch-zoom to read,** then scroll sideways to see the rest of each line.
2. **Turned up the phone's own text size** and expected the app to follow (iPhone: it does not).
3. **Read the icons without glasses:** ☆, ⓘ, ↗ and ▾ carry meaning with no words beside some of them.
4. **One-handed with a thumb:** the menu button is top right (42x42), the hardest corner to reach; everything is behind it.
5. **A family member sets it up in Simple mode and hands it over** (as with Jinny): no "keep it Simple" lock, so a stray tap reaches selling (known R4 rank 34).

## Push back / limits
- **Text size is bigger than P5's "S".** One setting is S only if done with one page-wide zoom; R6-2 shows names and boxes then need to wrap. Plan it as **M**: setting + fix the 5 places that break.
- **Simulated, not real phones.** Bigger text was simulated by scaling fonts; real Android font scale and iPhone zoom may differ a little. github.io can't be opened from here, so this is the repo copy.
- **I did not re-run R4/R5 items;** R5's small-target list (Show missing, Reorder, spot arrows, Fill spots boxes) still stands.
- **Which phone he and his testers use matters** for R6-1 (iPhone ignores the phone setting). Worth one line from him at planning.
