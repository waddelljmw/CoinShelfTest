# Test-user review 4: the heir, second run on v0.52.0 (session 22 re-run)

**How it was run:** a pretend heir who just inherited their uncle's coins opened a fresh, empty copy of the app (phone-sized screen, no internet, so live prices never load). They skipped the walk-through, then added **25 items through the real forms**: a storage box ("Uncle's cardboard box"), 3 raw Morgan/Peace dollars, 3 slabbed coins (NGC, PCGS, PCGS + CAC, with cert numbers), a Silver Eagle, 4 coins kept in the box, wheat cent, Indian Head, Franklin, Kennedy 90%, a roll of wheat cents, a 1964 proof set, a 10 oz Engelhard bar with serial number, a bag of junk silver, a casino token and a Canadian silver dollar. They also made a Whitman 9113 Lincoln wheat album (marked inherited, kept in "Uncle's desk drawer") and filled 6 of its 140 spots. Then they typed silver and copper prices when asked, marked all 23 coins inherited in one go ("Uncle Frank, 2024"), looked at Home, My Coins, Filter, Coin Value, My Stacks, Options and Needs info, built the Insurance Report with an owner name, tried Backup, Delete + Undo and the CSV export button. Done by script on a test computer.

**Not tested (don't trust this review on these):** printing or saving the report as a PDF (only the HTML file was created and read), photos, real touch feel, live metal prices, restoring a backup, importing a CSV, opening the exported CSV, an heir who wants to sell one coin, a box of hundreds of coins, Simple/Collector levels, the walk-through.

## App-store-style review: ★★★★☆
"Last time I got a $121 total and no warning. This time the app asked for the silver price the moment I added my first silver coin, and my report now says $1,446 with a clear 'estimate' note. I marked all my uncle's coins as inherited in one step and got a tidy report with his name, cert numbers and a note that it isn't an appraisal. Four stars: it took me a while to work out how to enter a bag of junk silver, a shoebox needed row-and-slot numbers I didn't have, and the report's order looked random."

## What was useful
- **Silver price pop-up** after the first silver coin ("Silver price needed", with a type-it-in box and "Later"), and a copper one after the first cent. Fixes the biggest complaint from the last heir review.
- **"Inherited" is remembered** for the next coin. After the first coin it needed no tap, so a plain coin took 6 to 7 entries (slabbed coins 10 to 12; the first coin 11 because of the price pop-up). Average over 19 coins: **7.8 entries**, 5 for a proof set.
- **Album spot = 3 taps per coin** (tap spot, "Add coin to this spot", Add coin). Year, type, Inherited, and the album's "Kept in" are already filled in.
- **Select all, then "Mark inherited…"** (From whom, When) changed 23 coins in one step with an Undo. "Needs info" fell from 25 to 17.
- **Insurance Report** has an owner name, totals (value, melt, count), an itemised list with cert numbers and where each coin is kept, a precious-metal table, how values were set, and a plain "not a professional appraisal" note.
- Home asks for a backup from the first coin; Delete shows an Undo toast and Undo worked.

## What was confusing or broke (no crashes, no script errors)
1. **Junk silver has no home.** Searching "junk" on Add coin finds nothing. The heir used Bar/Round → "Other silver item" and had to guess ounces and purity for a bag of dimes and quarters. An heir knows the face value ("$20 of dimes"), not the ounces. It lands in My Silver Stack as "20 oz Mixed dimes and quarters Silver item".
2. **A new cardboard box demands row and slot numbers.** Leaving them blank shows "Each tray needs 1–50 rows or columns and 1–100 slots in each." The way out is a "Numbered spots" switch lower on the form. A shoebox of coins shouldn't need it.
3. **Count mismatch.** Home said "25 coins need info", the list opened with "23 of 23 coins" (the 2 stack items aren't in My Coins). Home also says Coins 25 but "My Coins 21" plus a roll and a set.
4. **"Missing: grade" on every wheat cent and Indian Head.** An heir with 100 common cents can't grade them, so most of the list stays "needs info" and the "totals may be off" banner never clears.
5. **Report order looks random** (Silver Eagle, Buffalo nickel, bar, junk silver, Canada dollar…). Not by type, year, value or where kept. The **CAC sticker isn't on the report**, and "Uncle Frank, 2024" isn't either: only "Inherited" and the owner name show.
6. **Home still says "Cost $0.00 / Gain — Add prices paid"** for a collection that is entirely inherited.
7. **Coin page says "Do not sell" next to a "Mark as sold" button.** The rule "Inherited coins can't be For sale or Business" only showed in the Mark-inherited pop-up. I didn't find a way on the coin page to say "I'd like to sell this one eventually" (not tested further).
8. Single-coin **Delete has no "are you sure?"**, only the Undo toast.

## Business features
Not used (heir). Shows and Sales Reports are hidden from the menu until Seller Mode is on. That is right for this user.

## Ranked wish list
1. Add junk silver by **face value** (90%, 40%, 35%), with the ounces worked out for you.
2. Cardboard or "just a pile" box: **numbered spots off by default**.
3. Sort the report by type or year (let the user pick), and show **CAC** and **who/when inherited**.
4. A "can't grade these" choice for common circulated cents, so they stop counting as needing info.
5. Make "25 coins need info" and the list agree.
6. Hide Cost and Gain when everything is inherited.
7. A clear, plain "I'd like to sell this one eventually" on an inherited coin.
