# R7 solo Opus review — 2026-11-19 (app v0.95.2)

Scope: code merged since the 11-17 planning session (PR #272 → #324, steps 1–17 and fill-ins; 30 files, ~1,300 lines). Deep on what moves or saves coins (move between albums, swap, box slot, Fill spots + Undo, delete album, return to stock, Circulated / quick-add, backup/restore, CSV) and the step 14 money columns. Light on wording, layout and the demo. Reproduced problems only. Nothing fixed here.

## Findings

**R7-1 (High) — Moving a junk silver coin drops it out of its silver stack.**
`applyKept()` (boxes.js:74) clears `stack` for anything that isn't bullion. Three new one-tap moves call it: Move to another album (`slotMoveGo`, wants.js, v0.94.0), the upgrade swap (`swapGo`, old coin) and "+ on an empty box slot → a coin I have" (`boxHave`, v0.93.0).
Reproduced: a 1964 Kennedy half, grade Junk, in album 1 and in "My Silver Stack" → Move to another album → it's in album 2 and `stack` is now empty. The silver-stack total drops with no message; Undo works only while the toast shows. Album + stack together was allowed on purpose (blueprint decision 4).
Fix: in those three moves keep `stack` (only change where it sits). Small; no new saved field.

**R7-2 (Medium) — Tax CSV gain disagrees with the coin's profit when the buyer paid shipping.**
salesreport.js:153 (step 14): gain = sale price − fees − shipping paid − cost. It now subtracts the shipping you paid but never adds the shipping the buyer paid you (`shipCharged`). `saleProfit()` (selling.js:20) and the Sales report add it.
Example: sold $100 + $5 shipping charged, you paid $5 to ship, $10 fees, cost $50 → coin page profit $40, tax CSV gain $35. Selling waits for 2027, so not urgent, but it breaks step 13's "numbers agree".
Fix: add `shipCharged` to the gain (or show it as its own column). Small.

**R7-3 (Medium) — Fill spots can fill a $1 cost per coin without being asked.**
Fill spots now pre-fills Where from with the last source used (fillalbum.js, `lastFillSource`), and a blank price with a face-value source (Pocket change, Coin roll hunting, Bank roll) saves face as the price paid (fillalbum.js:136). So after one pocket-change album, the next Fill spots (say a Morgan album bought at a show) starts with "Pocket change"; any row left without a price saves $1.00 paid. Cost and gain look real but are wrong, and "needs info" won't flag them.
Fix: don't carry a face-value source into the next Fill spots (or carry it but don't auto-fill face unless that source was picked on this screen). Small.
