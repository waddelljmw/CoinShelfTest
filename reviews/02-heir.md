# Test-user review 2: the heir (session 22)

**How it was run:** a pretend heir who just inherited a box of coins opened a fresh, empty copy of the app (phone-sized screen). They added 10 coins (1881-S Morgan MS-63, 1922 Peace VF-20, and eight with no known grade: wheat cent, Mercury dime, silver Roosevelt dime, Franklin half, 1964 Kennedy half, Buffalo nickel, Indian Head cent, Jefferson nickel), marking each one's Source as inherited. Then they opened every page in the top-right menu, Options, entered a silver price, and built the Insurance Report with an owner name. Done by script on a test computer.

**Not tested (be careful trusting this review on these):** saving the report as a file and printing/PDF (file saving isn't available in the test), photos, real touch feel, live metal prices, backup/restore, Undo, selling an inherited coin, filling an album, CSV import, a big box of hundreds of coins.

## App-store-style review: ★★★☆☆
"I inherited my uncle's coins and wanted a list for the insurance company. The app understood right away that these were inherited: each coin says 'Inherited · do not sell', and the insurance report has my uncle's estate name, totals, a list of every coin and a note that it isn't an appraisal. That part is great. But my report said the whole box was worth $121, when my silver coins are worth far more, and I only found out because I typed in a silver price. Nothing told me to. I also couldn't see how to say 'I'd like to sell these eventually'. Three stars: the report is nearly what I need, but I wouldn't trust its total yet."

## What was useful
- **Source has an "Inherited" choice.** One pick marks the coin, hides "Paid" and gain math, and keeps it out of Cost and Gain on Home.
- About **8 taps/entries per coin** (+, US coin, type name, pick, year, mint mark if known, grade if known, open Purchase details, Source, Add coin). Unknown grade just stays "Raw".
- Insurance Report: owner name, date, coin IDs, grade, Red Book 2026 label on valued coins, totals by coin type, silver/copper weight, and the line "These values are the owner's estimates… not a professional appraisal. Some insurers require an appraisal for high-value coins."
- "Choose coins" lets the heir make a report for part of the box. Paid and Kept in columns are optional.
- Home asks for a backup right away ("You haven't saved a backup yet"). Good for an heir.
- Empty pages (Albums, Storage, Stacks, Want List) each say what to do next.

## What was confusing or broke (no crashes found)
1. **The report's total is too low until a silver price is entered, and nothing says so.** Test box: $121.32 total, Melt $0.00, "Melt values use spot prices of (none entered)". After typing silver at $30: total **$146.16**, Melt $72.45. For an insurance report the heir may send off, a silently low total is the biggest problem. (Same cause as review 1 finding 1.)
2. **Only 2 of 10 coins got a Red Book value** (Morgan, Peace). The other eight fell back to face value (a silver Franklin half shows $0.50, a Mercury dime $0.10). Possibly missing data, possibly correct for these dates and grades. **Check against the Red Book; nothing was guessed here.** Ungraded coins have no grade to look up, and nothing says "add a grade to get a value".
3. **"Paid (where recorded) $0.00"** appears on the report for coins that were inherited. There is no place to record what each coin was **worth when inherited**, which heirs often need for estates and taxes. (I'm not giving tax advice; this is just a field they will probably look for.)
4. **No way to mark an inherited coin For sale.** The coin page shows only Edit, Mark as sold, Delete; the form says "Inherited coins can't be set to For sale." Many heirs plan to sell some coins eventually. The only way found is to change Source to something else. That isn't obvious, and it would remove the inherited label.
5. **"Source" sits inside the folded "Purchase details" section.** An heir who didn't buy anything has no reason to open "Purchase details", so they may never mark the coin as inherited.
6. Words an heir won't know: *Raw, Do not sell (sounds like an order, not a label), Melt value, Red Book, Mint mark ("None (Philadelphia)"), Holder, Kept in*.
7. The locked "Fill in from photos · Coming soon" box is at the top of every Add coin form (same as review 1).
8. In the test, "Create report" closed the pop-up without a message. That is probably the missing file-saving in the test; check it on a real phone.

## Ranked wish list
1. Make the report honest: if any silver/gold coin has no price, warn on Home and on the report ("Totals are low: add a silver price"), and ask for the price when the first such coin is added. (Join with IDEAS 27.)
2. Say "No Red Book value for this date/grade" and "Add a grade to get a value" (IDEAS 29).
3. A "Value when inherited" field (and date) on inherited coins, with an optional column on the report.
4. Let an inherited coin be marked "For sale" (or "Ready to sell") without losing its inherited label.
5. Ask "Did you inherit these?" at the top of the Add coin form, or show Source outside the folded section.
6. Plain-words help for the words in finding 6 (IDEAS 31).
7. Hide the locked photo-fill note until it's usable.
