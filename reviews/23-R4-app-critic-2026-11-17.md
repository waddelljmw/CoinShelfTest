# R4 App critic: one ranked wish list (app v0.77.2, Tue 2026-11-17)

Report only. No app code, tests, PLAN.md or IDEAS.md were changed. I read reviews 18 to 22, IDEAS 99 to 116, and the app code behind every merge and every "done" belief below (file and line given). Nothing was run in a browser; where I only trust a review's run, the tag says so.

**Tags.** Size: **S** small fix · **M** a feature · **L** big, or changes how saved data is stored. **Model:** Sonnet for building, Opus for anything that reshapes saved data or needs a plan first. **Saved data?** Y = needs "ask first + 4-box check". **Evidence:** VERIFIED-code (I read the code) · VERIFIED-run (a sim reproduced it; I did not read the code) · GUESS.

## Verdict
- **Idea 99 is not truly closed.** The list *picker* bug is fixed, but the thing he first saw (his album says "wanted" for coins on the other album's list) and its twin (adding a coin crosses it off the *other* album's list) are both still in the code. One small fix; keep it at the top.
- **Three "OK" beliefs from review 19 are only partly true:** values match coin by coin, but **group totals disagree** (album value counts Business coins, Home does not); inherited protection has **two open doors** (editing a box into a for-sale case, editing a sold coin to Inherited); restore is fine on a fresh phone but **hides added album spots** when restoring over newer data.
- **Idea 108 is half done:** Photo fill is hidden in Options, but "🔒 Fill in from photos: coming soon" is still on every Add coin form.
- **"Create report crashed the browser":** I see no app-side crash cause. Most likely the test browser choking on an HTML download. Two cheap guards are worth adding (see Challenge 10).
- **Biggest cheap wins:** F1 (fast Back wipes every metal price), carry-over of Where from / Paid with / show (N1 + R3-1), Fill spots all ticked (P1), and the one-tap buttons next to Mark as sold.
