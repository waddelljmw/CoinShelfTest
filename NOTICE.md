Coin Cabinet is a personal project, written for one person's own use to keep track of his own coin collection. It is **not** a product, and it is **not offered for distribution, resale or use by others**. No license is granted to copy, share or reuse this code or its data.

Since version 0.65.0 this project contains no price or mintage figures. Prices are loaded on the owner's own phone from a file he keeps himself, typed from his own printed copy of *A Guide Book of United States Coins* (the "Red Book", Whitman Publishing) for his own reference. The only price list in the repository is a made-up one used by the tests. Dates, mint marks and varieties in `catalog/` are plain facts and carry no prices.
